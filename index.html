Gifts Inspired by Indian Art

A gift guide to MAP Shop, Museum of Art & Photography, Bengaluru

A good gift carries a story. The shop of the Museum of Art & Photography (MAP) adapts artworks and craft traditions from its collection into everyday objects, so each piece comes with an artist, a region or a technique behind it. Here are ideas by the kind of person you're buying for.

For the person who loves hosting

Pick something from MAP's tableware online range, or a pair of serving pieces from its designer bowls online India collection. Add a Kantha blue placemat set, inspired by Bengal's running stitch, and a runner such as the Plant Life table runner or the Jogen Chowdhury off-white table runner.

For a new home

Cushions are an easy housewarming gift. Try the Kalamkari cushion cover, the gentle Apple Tree cushion cover or the warm Orange Flower cushion cover. Round cushions like the Nankushiya blue round cushion and the Jamini cow green round cushion suit reading nooks.

For students and commuters

A sturdy bag is used every day. Browse tote bags online and pair one with a pouch from the bags & organisers online range. In the rainy season, one of MAP's designer umbrellas online is a gift that gets used straight away.

For families

Art jigsaw puzzles online from MAP turn a painting into a weekend activity everyone can join.
